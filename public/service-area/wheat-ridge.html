Permits for concrete, driveways and walls in Wheat Ridge

Who issues building permits in Wheat Ridge?

City of Wheat Ridge Building Division (Building and Inspection Services). Questions: (303) 235-2855, option 1; zoning questions go to the Planner of the Day.

Source: ci.wheatridge.co.us

What permit does driveway approach, curb or sidewalk work need in Wheat Ridge?

Any work in Wheat Ridge's right-of-way needs a ROW Excavation Permit or ROW Occupancy Permit from Public Works (curb, gutter and sidewalk work is not covered by a building permit), applied for through the City's OpenGov portal with a traffic control plan. The applicant needs a current Wheat Ridge Municipal Contractor License (Class A covers curb, gutter and sidewalk construction) backed by a $10,000 cash bond or approved performance bond, and work on Sheridan, Wadsworth, Kipling or Ward Rd also needs a CDOT permit.

Source: wheatridge.gov

When does a retaining wall need a permit in Wheat Ridge?

Wheat Ridge requires a building permit for retaining walls over 4 feet high or supporting a surcharge.

Source: ci.wheatridge.co.us

Water, sod and sprinkler rules in Wheat Ridge

Watering rules change with drought stages and seasons. These are the rules the water provider published as of October 13, 2026; check the linked page before you seed, sod or change a sprinkler schedule.

Who supplies water in Wheat Ridge?

Multiple providers: Denver Water, Consolidated Mutual Water Company, Valley Water District and Wheat Ridge Water District. Water lines are owned by special districts, and Denver Water is the main supplier for most of them; Wheat Ridge Water District serves most of the city east of Independence Court plus the area south of I-70 between Carr and Garrison, and Consolidated Mutual serves other parts of Wheat Ridge.

Source: wheatridge.gov · wrwdistrict.com

What are the outdoor watering rules in Wheat Ridge?

All four Wheat Ridge water providers follow Stage 1 drought rules: lawn watering two days a week (single-family even addresses Sunday/Thursday, odd Wednesday/Saturday, all others Tuesday/Friday) and none from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., with trees, shrubs and gardens allowed any day by hand or drip outside those hours. Wheat Ridge Water District has prohibited all lawn watering and lawn spray irrigation beginning Oct. 1, 2026.

Source: wheatridge.gov · wrwdistrict.com

Can new sod or seed get extra water in Wheat Ridge?

Wheat Ridge's restrictions page says new grass seed or sod is subject to the two-day watering schedule from June through August, and Wheat Ridge Water District says it will not grant any variance for new seed or sod.

Source: wheatridge.gov · wrwdistrict.com

Does a sprinkler backflow preventer need testing in Wheat Ridge?

Backflow testing is run by each water provider: Consolidated Mutual requires assemblies to be tested every year after installation, failed tests reported within 24 hours and fixed within 10 days, and accepts reports only through its portal (sprybackflow.cmwc.net). Denver Water requires annual tests by a certified tester, with reports emailed to crossconnectioncontrol@denverwater.org.

Source: cmwc.net · denverwater.org

Are there lawn replacement or sprinkler rebates in Wheat Ridge?

  • Denver Water rotary/high-efficiency sprinkler nozzle rebate — Customers of Denver Water or a qualifying provider (the list includes Valley Water District and Wheat Ridge Water District, but not Consolidated Mutual) can get up to $3 per nozzle for 10 to 100 eligible rotary nozzles (Hunter MP, Rain Bird, Toro Precision, K-Rain) that replace existing heads; one rebate per residence per 10 years, applied for online within 90 days of purchase and subject to available funds. denverwater.org

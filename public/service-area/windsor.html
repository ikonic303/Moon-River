Permits for concrete, driveways and walls in Windsor

Who issues building permits in Windsor?

Town of Windsor Community Development - Building Permits (building inspections by SAFEbuilt). Residential permits are applied for through the Town's Community Core portal and issued by the Town permit desk at 200 N. 11th St.; building inspections are requested through SAFEbuilt, while Town utility, curb and gutter inspections are requested from the Town.

Source: windsorco.gov

What permit does driveway approach, curb or sidewalk work need in Windsor?

Work in Town rights-of-way requires an Engineering right-of-way permit from Windsor Engineering and Public Works, and the Town is moving all right-of-way applications to a new OpenGov permit portal. Utility cuts, backfill and pavement patching must meet the Town's Street Specifications.

Source: windsorco.gov

Does a patio or driveway on private property need a building permit in Windsor?

Windsor's building permit requirements include a Soil Compaction Affidavit for concrete flatwork (Windsor Municipal Code Sec. 18-3-10). The Town's permit page doesn't say whether a stand-alone patio or driveway needs its own building permit, so ask the permit desk before you pour.

Source: windsorco.gov

Water, sod and sprinkler rules in Windsor

Watering rules change with drought stages and seasons. These are the rules the water provider published as of October 13, 2026; check the linked page before you seed, sod or change a sprinkler schedule.

Who supplies water in Windsor?

Town of Windsor (Windsor Water Utility); North Weld County Water District, City of Greeley and Fort Collins-Loveland Water District also named as providers. The Town's Water/Sewer page states that three separate water providers serve the Town of Windsor: North Weld County Water District, the City of Greeley and Fort Collins-Loveland Water District; NWCWD's service-area page says it serves the northern areas of Windsor. Some subdivisions have HOA non-potable irrigation systems, and residents contact their HOA about those.

Source: windsorco.gov · nwcwd.org

What are the outdoor watering rules in Windsor?

The Town's Efficiency page lists 'lawn watering restrictions (no watering from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. daily)' among its water-conservation measures; no day-of-week schedule or drought stage is posted on that page.

Source: windsorco.gov

Does a sprinkler backflow preventer need testing in Windsor?

Business and industrial Windsor Water customers, including sprinkler supply lines, must have backflow assemblies tested once a year by a certified tester (Backflow Solutions Inc. sends the notices) and retested after any repair or relocation. Testing of residential sprinkler backflow devices is voluntary.

Source: windsorco.gov

Are there lawn replacement or sprinkler rebates in Windsor?

  • Lawn Replacement Program (Windsor ColoradoScape Initiative pilot) — Pays $1-$2 per sq ft of living turf removed from a front yard (minimum 200 sq ft; maximum $2,000 per water account per year) for properties with a Town of Windsor water account (dual-use accounts excluded); designs must be approved before work starts and artificial turf is not eligible. 2026 applications ran Feb. 2-June 30, with projects completed and inspected by Oct. 16, 2026. windsorco.gov
  • Garden In A Box (Resource Central) — Windsor Utility customers get a $25 discount on Resource Central's pre-planned water-wise garden kits while limited supplies last; the program typically runs March through May. windsorco.gov

Also worth knowing in Windsor

  • A building permit is required to install a sprinkler system, whether it connects to Town potable water or a subdivision non-potable system. windsorco.gov
  • Under setback table 16-1-20(a), patios no more than 12 in. above grade may sit in any setback if at least 3 ft from the property line, and walls or retaining walls under 4 ft (top of wall to bottom of footer) may sit in any setback subject to fence and wall standards. windsorco.gov

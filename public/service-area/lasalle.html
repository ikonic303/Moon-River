Working in LaSalle

LaSalle is a community of roughly 2,359 residents, and LaSalle sits in Weld County. LaSalle sits along US-85, just south of Greeley, at about 4,680 feet. There is open ground on most sides and very little natural shade or windbreak: lawns dry out from the edges in, irrigation coverage matters more than irrigation volume, and a patio or driveway takes sun and wind from every direction.

Below is what LaSalle's own offices and water provider publish about permits, watering and backflow testing for the kind of work we do, checked October 13, 2026, with a link to each source.

Permits for concrete, driveways and walls in LaSalle

Who issues building permits in LaSalle?

Town of LaSalle (inspections by ProCode Inc.). Town Hall front-office staff process building permit applications (now through CommunityCore), and the Town contracts with ProCode Inc. (970-305-3161) for inspections.

Source: lasalletown.com

What permit does driveway approach, curb or sidewalk work need in LaSalle?

Building a sidewalk, curb or gutter requires a permit from the Town Clerk, with a fee and staking by the Town's engineer (Code Sec. 11-45); cutting any street or alley surface requires Town Clerk permission and a fee, and the cut must be patched within 10 days (Secs. 11-71, 11-72).

Source: library.municode.com

Does a patio or driveway on private property need a building permit in LaSalle?

The Town's Building Permits page lists patios, sprinkler systems and fences among projects that require a building permit; driveways and other flatwork are not addressed.

Source: lasalletown.com

When does a retaining wall need a permit in LaSalle?

LaSalle enforces the 2024 IRC (Code Sec. 18-143, Ord. 19-2025); its amendments to the IRC permit exemptions (R105.2) change only the shed and shingle-repair items, leaving the IRC retaining-wall exemption (walls up to 4 ft, measured from the bottom of the footing to the top of the wall, that do not support a surcharge) unchanged.

Source: library.municode.com

Water, sod and sprinkler rules in LaSalle

Watering rules change with drought stages and seasons. These are the rules the water provider published as of October 13, 2026; check the linked page before you seed, sod or change a sprinkler schedule.

Who supplies water in LaSalle?

Town of LaSalle. The Town owns the water, which Central Weld County Water District treats; the Dove Hill subdivision also has a Town non-potable water system.

Source: lasalletown.com

What are the outdoor watering rules in LaSalle?

Town water customers (except Dove Hill): odd addresses water Saturday, Monday and Wednesday, even addresses Sunday, Tuesday and Thursday, no watering on Fridays, and no watering 11 a.m. to 5 p.m. Dove Hill's non-potable system: odd addresses on odd days and even addresses on even days of the month, 15 to 20 minutes per zone.

Source: lasalletown.com

Can new sod or seed get extra water in LaSalle?

The Town's watering page lists no new-sod exemption; under Phase II drought restrictions, the Director of Public Works may issue permits for extended watering of new lawns (Code Sec. 13-40).

Source: library.municode.com

Does a sprinkler backflow preventer need testing in LaSalle?

LaSalle's Backflow Prevention Cross-Connection Control Program covers non-single-family connections (commercial, industrial, multi-family, agricultural and irrigation-system connections); single-family connections that pose no potential risk are exempt. Required RPZ assemblies must be tested and certified annually, with certificates uploaded to CommunityCore and emailed to backflow@safebuilt.com.

Source: lasalletown.com

Also worth knowing in LaSalle

  • Owners of property next to a street or sidewalk must maintain the trees and shrubs in the abutting right-of-way (Code Sec. 11-112). library.municode.com
  • If the Town orders an unsafe sidewalk repaired and the owner doesn't fix it within three days of notice, the Town can repair it and assess the adjacent owner (Code Sec. 11-53). library.municode.com
  • The Town charges a 2% use tax on materials, and permit fees may be doubled if work starts before the permit is issued. lasalletown.com

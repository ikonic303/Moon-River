Permits for concrete, driveways and walls in Foxfield

Who issues building permits in Foxfield?

SAFEbuilt (building permits for the Town of Foxfield). Foxfield's building permits are issued by SAFEbuilt through the Community Connect online portal (building official 303-941-4429), with zoning review by SAFEbuilt Studio and engineering review by the Town engineer, SEH. The Town does not require contractor licensing.

Source: townoffoxfield.colorado.gov

What permit does driveway approach, curb or sidewalk work need in Foxfield?

New driveways, culverts and temporary construction driveways need a Residential Driveway and Culvert Right-of-Way Use Permit reviewed by the Town Engineer ($250 fee plus a $500 engineering review deposit where applicable; 12–22 ft wide; culvert size set by the Town Engineer), and other work in the Town right-of-way needs a separate Right-of-Way permit. No permit is needed for improvements to the part of an existing driveway on private property.

Source: townoffoxfield.colorado.gov

Does a patio or driveway on private property need a building permit in Foxfield?

The Town lists concrete flatwork outside the Town right-of-way among projects that do not require a permit.

Source: townoffoxfield.colorado.gov

When does a retaining wall need a permit in Foxfield?

Retaining walls over 4 feet, measured from the bottom of the footing to the top of the wall, need a building permit and engineering review, and the Town engineer (SEH) reviews all retaining walls regardless of height.

Source: townoffoxfield.colorado.gov

Water, sod and sprinkler rules in Foxfield

Who supplies water in Foxfield?

Private wells (most homes); Arapahoe County Water & Wastewater Authority (ACWWA) central water available. Most Foxfield homes have private wells permitted by the State Engineer; a property can connect to ACWWA by buying a water tap ($32,228 in 2024) and transferring its deep water rights. All homes are on septic.

Source: townoffoxfield.colorado.gov

What are the outdoor watering rules in Foxfield?

The Town publishes no outdoor watering schedule; each private well's allowed uses and limits depend on its State Engineer permit, which owners can check with the Colorado Division of Water Resources (303-866-3581) or its well permit search.

Source: townoffoxfield.colorado.gov · dwr.colorado.gov

Also worth knowing in Foxfield

  • Residents are responsible for keeping their driveway culverts clear and repaired; clearing debris needs no permit, but repairs involving excavation or replacement need a right-of-way permit. townoffoxfield.colorado.gov
  • Lawn sprinkler installation is a One-Stop permit, and fences need a building permit (solid fences that may change drainage also get SEH engineering review). townoffoxfield.colorado.gov

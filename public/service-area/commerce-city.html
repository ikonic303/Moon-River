Permits for concrete, driveways and walls in Commerce City

Who issues building permits in Commerce City?

City of Commerce City Building Safety Division. Building Safety: 303-289-3790, cdpermits@c3gov.com, Civic Center, 7190 Colorado Blvd Ste 600.

Source: c3gov.com

What permit does driveway approach, curb or sidewalk work need in Commerce City?

Work in the public right-of-way needs a Right-of-Way (ROW) permit from Commerce City Public Works. All contractors must pull the permit and first get a Commerce City contractor's license with a certificate of insurance; apply through eTRAKiT at least one week ahead, and schedule inspections 24 hours in advance.

Source: c3gov.com

Does a patio or driveway on private property need a building permit in Commerce City?

Driveways and on-grade patios (including wood patios) are on the City's list of projects exempt from a building permit. Exempt projects must still meet building code and planning setback rules.

Source: c3gov.com

When does a retaining wall need a permit in Commerce City?

Retaining walls over 4 feet need a building permit. Walls 4 feet and under (measured from top of wall to bottom of footing) that do not support a surcharge are exempt.

Source: c3gov.com

Water, sod and sprinkler rules in Commerce City

Watering rules change with drought stages and seasons. These are the rules the water provider published as of October 13, 2026; check the linked page before you seed, sod or change a sprinkler schedule.

Who supplies water in Commerce City?

South Adams County Water & Sanitation District (SACWSD). The City states that water in Commerce City is provided by SACWSD, a separate special district, and that the city government does not provide water.

Source: southadamswaterco.gov · c3gov.com

What are the outdoor watering rules in Commerce City?

SACWSD declared a Stage 1 drought on May 13, 2026, limiting watering to two days a week: single-family odd addresses Wednesday and Saturday, even addresses Sunday and Thursday, and multifamily/commercial Tuesday and Friday. Outdoor watering is banned 10 a.m.–6 p.m. year-round; flowers and vegetables may be hand-watered with a shut-off hose any day, and residential fines are $100, $250, then $500 after a warning.

Source: conserve.southadamswaterco.gov

Can new sod or seed get extra water in Commerce City?

During Stage 1, new sod or seed may be watered for no more than 21 consecutive days, then no more than two days a week on the assigned schedule. SACWSD strongly discourages new sod June–August, lists April, May, September or October for new sod, and asks installers to notify it through its New Sod & Seed Installation form.

Source: conserve.southadamswaterco.gov

Does a sprinkler backflow preventer need testing in Commerce City?

SACWSD rules require backflow assemblies that CDPHE requires to be controlled, including irrigation assemblies, to be tested by a certified technician by Aug. 31 each year, with reports sent to the District (backflowreports@sacwsd.org); the District's backflow page describes annual testing as a requirement for commercial customers. Irrigation lines in new construction generally need a pressure vacuum breaker or reduced-pressure assembly, and atmospheric vacuum breakers are not allowed on irrigation systems.

Source: southadamswaterco.gov

Are there lawn replacement or sprinkler rebates in Commerce City?

  • Garden In A Box discount (Resource Central) — Commerce City residents get $25 off Resource Central low-water garden kits (kits from $132) through the City's Energy, Equity and Environment Division; 2026 discounts opened March 11, with pickup in May or June. c3gov.com

Also worth knowing in Commerce City

  • Property owners must keep the sidewalk next to their property maintained and hazard-free (City Code Sec. 6-2012). The City's 2026 Sidewalk Repair Program splits eligible public-sidewalk repairs 50/50; applications are closed and reopen in 2027. c3gov.com

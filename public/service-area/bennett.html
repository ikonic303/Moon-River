Permits for concrete, driveways and walls in Bennett

Who issues building permits in Bennett?

Town of Bennett Building Department (Community Development). Apply through the Town's SmartGov permit portal; 207 Muegge Way, 303-644-3249 ext. 1032, bennettbuilding@bennett.co.us.

Source: bennettco.gov

What permit does driveway approach, curb or sidewalk work need in Bennett?

A $75 Right-of-Way permit is needed for work in a Town right-of-way (street, sidewalk) or work that disrupts traffic: submit a site plan, traffic control plan and schedule, call 811, and notify Public Works 48 hours before starting (inspections can include forms/rebar and curb cuts). A separate $75 Access Permit is needed for a new driveway access point from a Town street or alley; access must be paved where it meets the right-of-way, with grades between 2% and 6%.

Source: bennettco.gov

Water, sod and sprinkler rules in Bennett

Watering rules change with drought stages and seasons. These are the rules the water provider published as of October 13, 2026; check the linked page before you seed, sod or change a sprinkler schedule.

Who supplies water in Bennett?

Town of Bennett (Public Works) – groundwater wells. The Town's 2026 Drinking Water Quality Report lists 11 groundwater wells in Adams County as the source.

Source: bennettco.gov

What are the outdoor watering rules in Bennett?

Standing rule: no watering 10 a.m.–6 p.m. (hand watering allowed). Mandatory Stage 1 restrictions took effect July 27, 2026, set to run through Sept. 8, 2026 unless modified: even addresses Monday/Wednesday/Friday, odd addresses Tuesday/Thursday/Saturday, a total of 4 watering hours per week, and no irrigation or hand watering 10 a.m.–6 p.m.

Source: bennettco.gov

Can new sod or seed get extra water in Bennett?

Residents who lay new seed or sod may apply to the Town for a special permit exempting them from the watering restrictions. The July 2026 Stage 1 notice also offers a temporary watering permit for new sod, seed or other qualifying landscaping.

Source: bennettco.gov

Also worth knowing in Bennett

  • Installing or replacing artificial turf requires a $25 Artificial Turf Permit with a site plan showing slope and drainage and a base-prep inspection before installation; repairs do not need a permit. bennettco.gov
  • The Town plans to raise water pressure in January 2027 when it moves to a single pressure zone, and offers a rebate of up to $550 for pressure reducing valves in eligible neighborhoods (Centennial, Brothers Four, Cordella, Old Town). bennettco.gov
  • Permit fees double if work begins before the building permit is obtained. bennettco.gov

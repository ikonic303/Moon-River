Permits for concrete, driveways and walls in Castle Pines

Who issues building permits in Castle Pines?

City of Castle Pines Building Department. The City issues building permits (applied for online through CommunityCore or on a paper application) and City staff perform inspections (303-705-0227, buildingdept@castlepinesco.gov); permits are valid 180 days.

Source: castlepinesco.gov

What permit does driveway approach, curb or sidewalk work need in Castle Pines?

Right-of-way permits are Public Works permits applied for through the City's SmartGov portal, with checklists and bonding requirements in SmartGov's Resources tab and a Right-of-Way Permit Bond Form on the City site. The 2026 fee schedule lists ROW fees such as an $82 base fee plus per-linear-foot charges for sidewalk and curb-and-gutter work, $8.75 per sq ft for paved street cuts and $375 for a 90-day dumpster placement.

Source: castlepinesco.gov

Water, sod and sprinkler rules in Castle Pines

Watering rules change with drought stages and seasons. These are the rules the water provider published as of October 13, 2026; check the linked page before you seed, sod or change a sprinkler schedule.

Who supplies water in Castle Pines?

Castle Pines North Metropolitan District (west of I-25); Parker Water & Sanitation District (east of I-25). The City says Castle Pines North Metropolitan District provides water and sewer to all residents and businesses west of I-25, and Parker Water & Sanitation District serves those east of I-25.

Source: castlepinesco.gov · cpnmd.specialdistrict.org

What are the outdoor watering rules in Castle Pines?

CPNMD's 2026 schedule allows watering three days a week, May through September, only after 8 p.m. and before 8 a.m. on assigned days (even addresses Sun/Tue/Thu, odd Mon/Wed/Sat, multifamily and commercial Mon/Wed/Fri); drip irrigation is allowed any time and water waste is prohibited. Homes east of I-25 follow PWSD's rules (no watering 10 a.m.–6 p.m.; even Sun/Tue/Thu, odd Mon/Wed/Sat).

Source: cpnmd.specialdistrict.org

Can new sod or seed get extra water in Castle Pines?

CPNMD customers may water new sod or plants daily under a 21-day exemption, avoiding 8 a.m.–8 p.m., and establishment is not permitted in July or August. PWSD customers east of I-25 need a PWSD sod/seed establishment permit during its restriction season.

Source: cpnmd.specialdistrict.org · pwsd.org

Does a sprinkler backflow preventer need testing in Castle Pines?

CPNMD requires annual testing by a certified technician of backflow devices on non-single-family properties (commercial buildings, HOAs, irrigation systems and fire suppression systems) and on single-family homes with a backflow device on a fire sprinkler system; it uses contractor BackFlow Secure, charges $12.50 a month per eligible account and set a March 31, 2026 deadline. PWSD's program applies east of I-25.

Source: cpnmd.specialdistrict.org · pwsd.org

Are there lawn replacement or sprinkler rebates in Castle Pines?

  • CPNMD Sod Replacement Rebate — Up to $3 per sq ft for residential lawn replacement (up to $2.50 for commercial/HOA); pre-approval of designs is strongly encouraged, and designs must include roughly 50% ColoradoScape plants. cpnmd.specialdistrict.org
  • CPNMD Smart Irrigation Controller rebate — $100 for a WaterSense-labeled smart controller, limit one per residential customer; rebates must be filed in the same calendar year. cpnmd.specialdistrict.org
  • CPNMD Rotator Sprinkler Nozzle rebate — $5 per nozzle for replacement rotator nozzles. cpnmd.specialdistrict.org
  • Garden In A Box discount (CPNMD) — $25 off a Resource Central Garden In A Box kit when Castle Pines North Metropolitan District is selected at checkout, while supplies last. cpnmd.specialdistrict.org
  • PWSD rebates (homes east of I-25) — PWSD customers can use PWSD's smart controller, rotary nozzle, rain sensor and spray-to-drip rebates, and $750 Resource Central turf removal discounts. pwsd.org

Also worth knowing in Castle Pines

  • Contractors must hold a City contractor license before working, and no permits are issued for unregistered contractors; classes include concrete and form ($75/yr), excavating including cuts into the right-of-way ($75/yr), asphalt paving ($75/yr) and single trades such as landscaping and lawn sprinkler systems ($50/yr). castlepinesco.gov
  • Installing a lawn sprinkler system requires a 1-Stop building permit, and fences over 7 feet need a plan-review permit. castlepinesco.gov

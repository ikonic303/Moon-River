Permits for concrete, driveways and walls in Lakewood

Who issues building permits in Lakewood?

City of Lakewood permit counter (building permits and inspections). Lakewood handles permitting and inspections through eTRAKiT and the permit counter at 470 S. Allison Pkwy (PermitCounter@LakewoodCO.gov, 303-987-7500).

Source: lakewoodco.gov

What permit does driveway approach, curb or sidewalk work need in Lakewood?

Lakewood permits and inspects concrete work in the public way, including sidewalks, curb and gutter, curb ramps, driveways and driveway aprons, and only contractors with a Class D Municipal registration may take permits for right-of-way work (the Concrete 5A class does not cover the public way). For right-of-way permit questions, the City says to contact an engineering plans reviewer at 303-987-7500.

Source: lakewoodco.gov

Does a patio or driveway on private property need a building permit in Lakewood?

Lakewood's list of permit exemptions says private walks and driveways no more than 30 inches above grade, not over a basement or story below and not part of an accessible route, do not need a building permit; patio covers do require one.

Source: lakewoodco.gov

When does a retaining wall need a permit in Lakewood?

In Lakewood, retaining walls up to 30 inches tall (measured from grade to the top of the wall) are exempt unless they support a surcharge or impound flammable liquids; retaining walls taller than 30 inches require a permit.

Source: lakewoodco.gov

Water, sod and sprinkler rules in Lakewood

Watering rules change with drought stages and seasons. These are the rules the water provider published as of October 13, 2026; check the linked page before you seed, sod or change a sprinkler schedule.

Who supplies water in Lakewood?

Multiple providers (nearly 20), most buying water from Denver Water. Lakewood's provider list includes Consolidated Mutual Water Company, Denver Water, the City's own Lakewood Water and Sewer Utility, and the Green Mountain, Alameda, Bancroft-Clover, Bear Creek, Bonvue and Lakehurst water and sanitation districts, among others; the City's My Neighborhood tool shows the provider for an address.

Source: lakewoodco.gov

What are the outdoor watering rules in Lakewood?

Most Lakewood water providers follow Denver Water's Stage 1 drought rules: lawn watering two days a week by address, only between 6 p.m. and 10 a.m.; the City's Aug. 26 update says lawn watering and all spray irrigation are prohibited after Sept. 30, 2026, while trees and plants may still be watered by hand or drip. Some providers may set stricter rules, so residents should confirm with their own provider.

Source: lakewoodco.gov · jeffco.us

Does a sprinkler backflow preventer need testing in Lakewood?

Backflow testing is run by each water provider: Consolidated Mutual requires assemblies to be tested every year after installation and accepts reports only through its portal (sprybackflow.cmwc.net), and Denver Water requires annual tests by a certified tester with reports emailed to crossconnectioncontrol@denverwater.org.

Source: cmwc.net · denverwater.org

Are there lawn replacement or sprinkler rebates in Lakewood?

  • Denver Water rotary/high-efficiency sprinkler nozzle rebate — Customers of Denver Water or a qualifying provider serving Lakewood (the list includes the City of Lakewood and the Alameda, Bancroft-Clover, Bonvue, Green Mountain and Lakehurst water and sanitation districts, but not Consolidated Mutual) can get up to $3 per nozzle for 10 to 100 eligible rotary nozzles (Hunter MP, Rain Bird, Toro Precision, K-Rain) that replace existing heads; one rebate per residence per 10 years, applied for online within 90 days of purchase and subject to available funds. denverwater.org

Also worth knowing in Lakewood

Permits for concrete, driveways and walls in Dacono

Who issues building permits in Dacono?

City of Dacono Department of Community Development (Building Division). Building department services are contracted to SAFEbuilt Colorado, and permits go through Community Core.

Source: daconoco.gov

What permit does driveway approach, curb or sidewalk work need in Dacono?

An excavation permit from Public Works, Engineering and Water is required before making or changing sidewalks, driveways, curbs or gutters, or doing any other work on public property, easements or rights-of-way. The City's instructions list a $500 permit fee, a bond of $2,500 or $76 per square yard (whichever is greater) and liability insurance naming the City.

Source: daconoco.gov

Water, sod and sprinkler rules in Dacono

Watering rules change with drought stages and seasons. These are the rules the water provider published as of October 13, 2026; check the linked page before you seed, sod or change a sprinkler schedule.

Who supplies water in Dacono?

City of Dacono (Department of Public Works, Engineering and Water). The City supplies treated water to all customers within city limits. Central Weld County Water District treats that water and delivers it to the City.

Source: daconoco.gov

What are the outdoor watering rules in Dacono?

No mandatory watering restrictions are in place. The City asks residents to conserve voluntarily by checking irrigation systems for leaks and cutting a few minutes from each zone.

Source: daconoco.gov

Can new sod or seed get extra water in Dacono?

On May 11, 2026 the City adopted a WaterWise Landscape Code (Ordinance No. 1016) that requires landscape and irrigation plans and limits turf grass.

Source: daconoco.gov

Does a sprinkler backflow preventer need testing in Dacono?

The City or its contractor performs annual backflow inspections at $100 per device, billed on the water bill. The program covers commercial, industrial, multifamily, HOA, fire and non-single-family irrigation services, and does not apply to single-family residential properties.

Source: daconoco.gov

Also worth knowing in Dacono

  • Under its Sidewalk Repair Program, the City inspects and contracts repairs for damaged public sidewalks, such as vertical displacement over 3/4 inch or cracks over 1 inch wide. Residents can submit repair requests. daconoco.gov
  • For 811 locates, the City marks only its water mains and the service line to the meter. Owners must locate their own service line from the meter to the house. daconoco.gov

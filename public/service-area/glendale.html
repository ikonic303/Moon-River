Who supplies water in Glendale?

City of Glendale water utility (Public Works), supplied by Denver Water. Glendale buys all its water from Denver Water under a master-meter contract; the City maintains the water mains and bills customers.

Source: glendale.co.us

What are the outdoor watering rules in Glendale?

To align with Denver Water's 2026 drought response, Glendale requires multifamily, commercial and HOA properties to water only on Tuesdays and Fridays, and raised water rates 5% from May 1 until further notice.

Source: glendale.co.us

Does a sprinkler backflow preventer need testing in Glendale?

Glendale and Denver Water require a backflow device on every water service in Glendale (irrigation typically needs a pressure vacuum breaker or reduced-pressure assembly), installed under city inspection. Devices must be tested annually by a certified technician at the owner's expense, with the report sent to Glendale Public Works (601 S. Elm St.) and records kept three years; failed tests must be reported immediately (303-639-4500).

Source: glendale.co.us

Are there lawn replacement or sprinkler rebates in Glendale?

  • Denver Water rotary/high-efficiency sprinkler nozzle rebate — Up to $3 per nozzle for 10 to 100 qualifying rotary nozzles (Hunter MP, Rain Bird, Toro Precision, K-Rain), once per residence every 10 years; replacements only (not new construction), applied for online within 90 days of purchase. The City of Glendale is on Denver Water's list of qualifying providers. denverwater.org

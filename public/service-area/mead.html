Permits for concrete, driveways and walls in Mead

Who issues building permits in Mead?

Town of Mead Building Division (inspections contracted to SAFEbuilt Colorado). The Building Division reviews applications, issues permits and enforces building codes, and the Town contracts with SAFEbuilt Colorado for building inspections.

Source: meadco.gov

What permit does driveway approach, curb or sidewalk work need in Mead?

Anyone wanting a driveway or curb cut onto a Town road must contact the Town; new driveways need a Street, Driveway & Culvert Permit ($250 for a driveway or culvert, generally one access per parcel, with liability insurance and a two-year warranty on right-of-way work). Any excavation or work in a street, alley, sidewalk or public place needs a Utility Excavation, Street Cut & Obstruction Permit ($50 base fee plus road-cut, inspection and use-tax charges), and the Town Engineer oversees right-of-way permitting.

Source: meadco.gov

Does a patio or driveway on private property need a building permit in Mead?

The Town's 'Do I Need a Building Permit?' page lists flatwork (patios, sidewalks and driveways) under 'Permit Not Required', but advises checking with the Building Division about specific code requirements.

Source: meadco.gov

Water, sod and sprinkler rules in Mead

Watering rules change with drought stages and seasons. These are the rules the water provider published as of October 13, 2026; check the linked page before you seed, sod or change a sprinkler schedule.

Who supplies water in Mead?

Little Thompson Water District and Longs Peak Water District. The Town's Water Service page says water service in Mead is provided by Little Thompson Water District (ltwd.org) and Longs Peak Water District (lpwd.org); it does not say which areas each district serves.

Source: meadco.gov

What are the outdoor watering rules in Mead?

Little Thompson Water District customers are under a Water Shortage Watch as of April 2026: water only before 10 a.m. or after 6 p.m., at most three days a week (even addresses Monday, Wednesday, Saturday; odd addresses Tuesday, Thursday, Sunday), with trees and shrubs on drip, hand watering, deep-root needle or arbor bag. Violations draw a written warning, then fines of $100, $200 and $300.

Source: littlethompsonwd.gov

Can new sod or seed get extra water in Mead?

Under LTWD's drought matrix, the new-lawn permit provision is not applicable at the Watch level in effect as of April 2026; at Levels I and II, new lawns from seed or sod need an LTWD permit (June 1-Sept. 15) that allows watering any time and day as defined in the permit, and at Level III new lawns are not allowed.

Source: littlethompsonwd.gov

Does a sprinkler backflow preventer need testing in Mead?

LTWD requires backflow assemblies on sprinkler and irrigation systems; non-residential customers, HOAs and residential customers with high-hazard connections must have devices tested annually by a certified tester and submit reports to LTWD. Standard home sprinkler systems are not considered high hazard, so residential test reporting is not required, though LTWD recommends a pressure vacuum breaker tested annually.

Source: littlethompsonwd.gov

Are there lawn replacement or sprinkler rebates in Mead?

  • Lawn Replacement Program (LTWD with Resource Central) — LTWD customers removing at least 200 sq ft of healthy turf can get up to $750 off Resource Central's lawn removal ($1 instead of $2.75 per sq ft, up to 454 sq ft) or toward up to four Garden In A Box kits for DIY projects. The 2026 program is closed; a 2027 interest list is open. littlethompsonwd.gov
  • Soil Amendment Rebate (LTWD) — Up to $500 back for adding at least 3 cubic yards of compost or other amendment per 1,000 sq ft, loosening the top 8 in. of soil and mixing the amendment into the top 4-6 in. littlethompsonwd.gov
  • Water-Saving Plants & Trees Rebate (LTWD) — Up to $300 back on eligible xeric or drought-resistant plants and trees, submitted with an itemized receipt. littlethompsonwd.gov
  • Smart Irrigation Controller Rebate (LTWD) — Up to $125 back for a WaterSense-labeled irrigation controller. littlethompsonwd.gov
  • Slow the Flow sprinkler evaluations (LTWD with Resource Central) — Free 75-minute sprinkler evaluations for LTWD customers; the 2026 season is closed and a 2027 interest list is open. littlethompsonwd.gov
  • Garden In A Box (LTWD with Resource Central) — $25 discount for LTWD customers on water-wise garden kits; 2026 discounts are sold out. littlethompsonwd.gov

Also worth knowing in Mead

Below is what Thornton's own offices and water provider publish about permits, watering and backflow testing for the kind of work we do, checked October 13, 2026, with a link to each source.

Permits for concrete, driveways and walls in Thornton

Who issues building permits in Thornton?

City of Thornton Building Inspection Division (City Development – Building Division). Apply through the CityView Portal; 303-538-7250, buildings@thorntonco.gov, 9500 Civic Center Dr.

Source: thorntonco.gov

What permit does driveway approach, curb or sidewalk work need in Thornton?

Work in Thornton's public right-of-way needs an Infrastructure Right-of-Way construction permit from Public Works Engineering Services. These permits go only to contractors with a Thornton contractor license, are applied for online at permits.thorntonco.gov (about 14 business days' review, plus a traffic control permit) and require a pre-construction meeting at least 48 hours before work. Cut sidewalk must be replaced by the full panel, and the page says the City does not allow curb cuts because no ADA-compliant specifications are approved.

Source: thorntonco.gov

Does a patio or driveway on private property need a building permit in Thornton?

Concrete sidewalks on private property and concrete porches without roofs do not need a building permit. New residential driveway permits have been filed with the Planning & Zoning Division since Oct. 28, 2024, through the CityView portal ('Apply for a Planning Permit').

Source: thorntonco.gov

When does a retaining wall need a permit in Thornton?

Retaining walls over 48 inches from the bottom of the footing need a building permit, with engineering required. Walls 48 inches or less that do not support a surcharge are exempt, and retaining walls qualify for the Building Division's homeowner rapid review.

Source: thorntonco.gov

Water, sod and sprinkler rules in Thornton

Watering rules change with drought stages and seasons. These are the rules the water provider published as of October 13, 2026; check the linked page before you seed, sod or change a sprinkler schedule.

Who supplies water in Thornton?

City of Thornton (Thornton Water, Infrastructure Department). The City's drought permit form says the permit is only for Thornton water customers and tells sewer-only customers to contact their water provider, so some properties with Thornton sewer service get water from another provider.

Source: thorntonco.gov

What are the outdoor watering rules in Thornton?

Stage 1 drought (effective March 15, 2026) limits lawn watering to two days a week, and customers choose their two days; lawn watering is banned 10 a.m.–6 p.m. Trees, shrubs, perennials, annuals and vegetable gardens may be watered any day, and lawn watering under Stage 1 rules continues in October.

Source: thorntonwater.com

Can new sod or seed get extra water in Thornton?

Thornton discourages new sod or seed during the drought. A Drought Irrigation Permit allows extra watering for 21 days for new sod or seed ($50) or 42 days for native/warm-season grass seed ($100); permits are offered for May, June, September and October installs (none for July or August) and only to Thornton water customers.

Source: thorntonwater.com · forms.thorntonco.gov

Does a sprinkler backflow preventer need testing in Thornton?

Installing a backflow preventer for a new lawn sprinkler system needs a building permit, issued without plan review. Thornton's program requires backflow assemblies to be tested by a certified technician at installation and at least yearly, with results filed online through Backflow Solutions Inc. (BSI Online); the city describes the annual-inspection requirement for commercial, industrial and multifamily (3+ unit) properties.

Source: thorntonco.gov

Are there lawn replacement or sprinkler rebates in Thornton?

  • Water-Wise Landscape Rebate — Up to $2 per sq ft for converting grass or a problem area to a low-water garden (2026 rules); all-rock projects with no plants do not qualify. thorntonwater.com
  • Lawn Replacement Program (Resource Central) — Remove at least 200 sq ft of lawn: the 2026 removal service is $2.75 per sq ft, with the City paying the first $1.75 per sq ft up to $750. The DIY option (up to 4 free Garden In A Box kits) filled for 2026. thorntonwater.com
  • Irrigation Controller Rebate — Up to $200 for a WaterSense-certified, weather-based, Wi-Fi irrigation controller, one per 10 years. thorntonwater.com
  • Rain Sensor Rebate — $25 for a residential rain sensor connected to a sprinkler system, one per 10 years. thorntonwater.com
  • Artificial Turf Rebate — A 2026 residential artificial turf rebate is offered; the amount and rules are in the City's 2026 rules document. thorntonwater.com
  • Slow the Flow sprinkler evaluation (Resource Central) — Free sprinkler evaluation with a custom watering schedule for Thornton Water customers; the 2026 program is full and a 2027 interest list is open. thorntonwater.com

Also worth knowing in Thornton

  • The City's Concrete Replacement Program ($140,000 a year) replaces broken or damaged concrete, prioritized by pedestrian hazard, structural damage, surface wear and drainage impact; work generally starts in March. thorntonco.gov

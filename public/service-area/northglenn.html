Permits for concrete, driveways and walls in Northglenn

Who issues building permits in Northglenn?

City of Northglenn Building Division (building services provided by SAFEbuilt). The Building Division is in City Hall, 11701 Community Center Dr.; call 303-450-8745 to reach the SAFEbuilt representative.

Source: northglenn.org

What permit does driveway approach, curb or sidewalk work need in Northglenn?

Any work by a private company on City-owned or controlled ground (streets, sidewalks, medians, easements) needs a Right-of-Way permit from Public Works' Right-of-Way Division (row@northglenn.org), applied for online with contractor registration and a bond; public sidewalk or curb-and-gutter replacement is also listed as permit work handled by Public Works. Concrete replacement in the right-of-way requires an approved traffic control plan, form inspections with 24 hours' notice, and soils testing before the pour.

Source: northglenn.org

Does a patio or driveway on private property need a building permit in Northglenn?

Concrete private sidewalks and driveways and concrete or stone patio slabs without roofs do not need a building permit. Public sidewalk or curb-and-gutter replacement does need a permit, through Public Works.

Source: northglenn.org

When does a retaining wall need a permit in Northglenn?

Retaining walls 4 feet or more, measured from the bottom of the footing to the top of the wall, need a building permit.

Source: northglenn.org

Water, sod and sprinkler rules in Northglenn

Watering rules change with drought stages and seasons. These are the rules the water provider published as of October 13, 2026; check the linked page before you seed, sod or change a sprinkler schedule.

Who supplies water in Northglenn?

City of Northglenn water utility (Public Works / Water Resources).

Source: northglenn.org

What are the outdoor watering rules in Northglenn?

Stage 2 mandatory restrictions: odd addresses water Wednesday and Saturday, even addresses Thursday and Sunday, and multifamily, commercial, HOA and park properties Tuesday and Friday, with no lawn watering on Mondays; lawn irrigation is allowed only 6 p.m.–10 a.m. Maximum run times per zone apply (for example, in July: 24 minutes for fixed spray, 52 for rotor, 65 for rotary heads), and trees, shrubs and gardens may be watered any time by shut-off hose, can or drip.

Source: northglenn.org

Can new sod or seed get extra water in Northglenn?

Under the 2026 rules, new sod or seed is allowed only on newly developed commercial properties and residential properties never landscaped before, with limited exceptions by application. A free permit allows watering new sod for up to 21 days, applied for before installation with the sod/seed receipt; sod repairs under 16 sq ft may be hand-watered any time.

Source: northglenn.org

Does a sprinkler backflow preventer need testing in Northglenn?

A building permit is needed when a lawn sprinkler backflow device is installed or replaced, and the City generally requires reduced-pressure (RP) backflow assemblies on irrigation lines, with some exceptions. Annual testing by an ABPA- or ASSE-certified tester is required for commercial, industrial and non-single-family residences, with reports filed through BSIonline.com.

Source: northglenn.org

Are there lawn replacement or sprinkler rebates in Northglenn?

  • Irrigation Timer Rebate — Up to $100 for a WaterSense-labeled irrigation timer, one per address every 10 years (Northglenn water customers; credited to the water bill). northglenn.org
  • Rotary Sprinkler Head Rebate — $3 per rotary sprinkler head, up to 25 per address. northglenn.org
  • Slow the Flow & Smart Controller Program (Resource Central) — Free irrigation checkup; participants may also qualify for a free Rachio smart controller and Rain Bird rain sensor (call 303-999-3824). northglenn.org
  • Lawn Replacement (Resource Central) — Lawn removal service discounted up to $750, or a DIY option with up to 4 Garden In A Box kits at no cost. northglenn.org
  • Garden In A Box (Resource Central) — $25 discount for Northglenn residents on xeriscape garden kits; sales open in March and June. northglenn.org

Also worth knowing in Northglenn

  • Call 811 (Colorado 811/UNCC) before digging to have City water and sewer lines and other utilities marked; except in emergencies, allow 48 hours for the response. northglenn.org
  • Contractors hired by a homeowner must be licensed with the City and pull the permits; homeowners permitting work on their own dwelling do not need a contractor's license. northglenn.org

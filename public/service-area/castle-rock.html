Permits for concrete, driveways and walls in Castle Rock

Who issues building permits in Castle Rock?

Town of Castle Rock Development Services, Building Division (Building Counter). Building permits are applied for and inspections scheduled through the Town's eTRAKiT portal (Building Counter: buildingcounter@crgov.com, 720-733-3527), and the Town's FAQ says to email the Building Counter with a project description to learn which permits are needed. The Town enforces the 2024 ICC codes for permit applications submitted on or after June 30, 2026.

Source: etrakitt9.crgov.com · crgov.com

Water, sod and sprinkler rules in Castle Rock

Watering rules change with drought stages and seasons. These are the rules the water provider published as of October 13, 2026; check the linked page before you seed, sod or change a sprinkler schedule.

Who supplies water in Castle Rock?

Castle Rock Water (Town of Castle Rock water utility). Castle Rock Water (175 Kellogg Court, 720-733-6000) provides water service and runs the CRconserve.com conservation site.

Source: crgov.com

What are the outdoor watering rules in Castle Rock?

Castle Rock Water's mandatory residential schedule runs May 1–Sept. 30: water every third day only before 8 a.m. or after 8 p.m., on days set by the last digit of the address (0–3 square, 4–6 circle, 7–9 diamond), while nonresidential properties water three days a week from midnight to 8 a.m. (west of I-25 Mon/Wed/Fri, east Tue/Thu/Sat). The schedule is not currently in effect, but a Drought Stage 1 advisory asks all customers to cut use 10% voluntarily, and repeat residential violations bring $25, $50, $100 and then $200 surcharges after a first warning.

Source: crconserve.com

Can new sod or seed get extra water in Castle Rock?

A $9 temporary watering exemption, which can be requested the day of installation, lets new sod be watered any day for 21 days and new seed or plants for 30 days, still only before 8 a.m. or after 8 p.m.; exemptions are not issued in July. Per the Town FAQ (updated Dec. 27, 2023), Kentucky bluegrass has been banned from new development since 2018, and new homes may not have front-yard turf, with limits on back-yard turf.

Source: crconserve.com · crgov.com

Does a sprinkler backflow preventer need testing in Castle Rock?

The Town's Landscape and Irrigation Criteria Manual (Apr. 23, 2026) requires a passing backflow prevention assembly test within 10 days of irrigation system start-up each season and before a certificate of occupancy; the manual does not say where test reports are filed.

Source: crgov.com

Are there lawn replacement or sprinkler rebates in Castle Rock?

  • Residential ColoradoScape Renovation rebate — $3.25 per sq ft ($1.00 per sq ft for hardscape) for replacing at least 400 sq ft, or a whole front yard, of healthy high-water turf, up to 1,500 sq ft, with a pre-approved application and initial inspection; artificial turf does not qualify. The 2026 program is closed, and 2027 details are due in April 2027. crconserve.com
  • Rotary Nozzle Retrofit rebate — Up to $5 per rotary nozzle that replaces a fixed-spray nozzle; equipment must be bought and installed and the application filed in the same rebate year. crconserve.com
  • Garden In A Box discount — Castle Rock Water customers may qualify for a $25 discount on Resource Central Garden In A Box xeric garden kits. crconserve.com

Also worth knowing in Castle Rock

  • Castle Rock Water requires rain sensors on all irrigation systems. crconserve.com
  • Hand watering (hose held in hand) is allowed any day and time, and irrigation maintenance may run any time while a person is actively attending it. crconserve.com

Permits for concrete, driveways and walls in Garden City

Who issues building permits in Garden City?

Town of Garden City (building inspections by ProCode Inc.). The Town takes building permit applications through its CommunityCore portal and contracts with ProCode Inc. for building inspections; Town Hall is (970) 351-0041.

Source: townofgardencity.com

What permit does driveway approach, curb or sidewalk work need in Garden City?

Town Code Sec. 11-2-10 requires a permit before building, altering or repairing any sidewalk, curb, gutter, driveway or curb cut in the public right-of-way, and Sec. 11-2-30 requires Town approval for any driveway or curb cut that accesses a public street. Apply to the Town Clerk and pay the permit fee and refundable deposit set in the Town fee schedule (Secs. 11-2-90, 11-2-100).

Source: library.municode.com

Does a patio or driveway on private property need a building permit in Garden City?

The Town's permit chart lists sidewalks not more than 30 inches above grade and replacement of an existing driveway as exempt from a building permit; new driveways are not addressed (a driveway or curb cut onto a public street needs Town approval under Code Sec. 11-2-30).

Source: townofgardencity.com

When does a retaining wall need a permit in Garden City?

A building permit is required for retaining walls over 4 ft tall; walls under 4 ft are exempt.

Source: townofgardencity.com

Water, sod and sprinkler rules in Garden City

Watering rules change with drought stages and seasons. These are the rules the water provider published as of October 13, 2026; check the linked page before you seed, sod or change a sprinkler schedule.

Who supplies water in Garden City?

City of Greeley Water and Sewer. The Town's utility page lists the City of Greeley (970-350-9811) for water and sewer service.

Source: townofgardencity.com

What are the outdoor watering rules in Garden City?

Garden City is served by Greeley Water, whose 2026 rules apply to all Greeley water customers: lawn watering April 15 to October 31, no watering 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., and residential customers not on a water budget limited to three days a week (even addresses Sun/Tue/Thu, odd addresses Mon/Wed/Sat). Customers on a water budget may water any day, and commercial, multifamily and HOA customers water Tue/Fri/Sun.

Source: greeleyco.gov

Can new sod or seed get extra water in Garden City?

Greeley Water requires a free Lawn Installation Variance to install, replace, patch or renovate 250 sq ft or more of lawn, with 4 cubic yards of compost per 1,000 sq ft for bluegrass-type turf; the variance allows extra watering for 30 days with a raised water budget.

Source: greeleyco.gov

Does a sprinkler backflow preventer need testing in Garden City?

A Town building permit is required to install a lawn sprinkler backflow prevention device. Garden City's water comes from Greeley, whose annual backflow testing program (Greeley Code Sec. 20-191) covers commercial, industrial and multi-unit residential connections rather than single-family homes.

Source: townofgardencity.com · library.municode.com

Are there lawn replacement or sprinkler rebates in Garden City?

  • Residential Landscape Improvement and Water Rebate Program — Rebates half of a household's May to August water bills, up to $400 per the Town's application form (the code default is $300 unless the Board sets another amount), one per household; apply by the last business day in May with a before photo and planned landscape improvements, then submit photos and summer bills by September 15. Offered only in years the Board of Trustees funds it (Code Art. 5). townofgardencity.com
  • Residential Revitalization Grant — Reimburses up to 50% of approved exterior improvement costs, including landscaping, up to $3,000 per property per year; requires two contractor estimates and Board of Trustees approval, with work finished within five months. townofgardencity.com

Also worth knowing in Garden City

  • Owners or occupants must keep adjoining sidewalks in good repair and clear snow from residential sidewalks within 24 hours after a storm (Code Secs. 11-1-10, 11-1-20). library.municode.com
  • Tree Care and Removal Assistance Program: the Town may pay up to 80% of proper tree care, capped at $800 per applicant, for work pre-approved by the Town Clerk and done by a tree company licensed in Greeley or Evans; apply before November 10 (Code Art. 6). library.municode.com

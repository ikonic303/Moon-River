Permits for concrete, driveways and walls in Evans

Who issues building permits in Evans?

City of Evans Building Division. The Building Division handles plan review, building permits and inspections; applications are submitted through the City's CitizenServe portal.

Source: evanscolorado.gov

What permit does driveway approach, curb or sidewalk work need in Evans?

Evans Engineering reviews and issues permits for all work in the public right-of-way (Code 12.04.040), and any new private driveway access to a public street needs an Access Permit from Public Works (Code 12.10.020). Residential curb cuts are limited to 24 ft with at least 10 ft between cuts (Code 12.08.050), and excavation permits must be pulled by a bonded contractor at least 48 hours before work.

Source: evanscolorado.gov · library.municode.com

Does a patio or driveway on private property need a building permit in Evans?

The City's Building FAQs list concrete flatwork as not requiring a building permit, but note that driveway expansions may require an Access Permit.

Source: evanscolorado.gov

When does a retaining wall need a permit in Evans?

Evans enforces the 2018 International Residential Code (Code 15.09.010); its local amendments to the IRC permit exemptions (R105.2) change only the shed, fence (6 ft) and shingle-repair items, leaving the IRC retaining-wall exemption (walls up to 4 ft, measured from the bottom of the footing to the top of the wall, that do not support a surcharge) unchanged. City handouts do not restate the threshold.

Source: library.municode.com

Water, sod and sprinkler rules in Evans

Watering rules change with drought stages and seasons. These are the rules the water provider published as of October 13, 2026; check the linked page before you seed, sod or change a sprinkler schedule.

Who supplies water in Evans?

City of Evans water utility (Public Works and Utilities). The City runs the water utility. Some developments get non-potable irrigation water from the Evans Ditch system or other ditch companies, with the City supplying potable water (at potable rates) until ditch water is available.

Source: evanscolorado.gov

What are the outdoor watering rules in Evans?

Under the drought emergency and Emergency Ordinance 860-26 adopted April 21, 2026, outdoor watering is allowed April 15 to October 15 only on assigned days and never 10 a.m. to 6 p.m.: even addresses Sun/Tue/Thu, odd addresses Mon/Wed/Sat, multifamily and commercial Sun/Tue/Fri.

Source: evanscolorado.gov

Can new sod or seed get extra water in Evans?

Evans Code 13.04.200(A)(3)(b) normally lets newly seeded or sodded lawns under three months old be watered any day at any time, but the code says drought-emergency restrictions supersede that section, and the City's 2026 drought pages do not mention a new-lawn exemption. Confirm with Evans Customer Service (970-475-1170) before relying on it.

Source: library.municode.com

Does a sprinkler backflow preventer need testing in Evans?

Evans' cross-connection rules (Code ch. 13.24) cover commercial, industrial and multi-family connections and do not apply to single-family connections unless the City finds a cross connection there. Required assemblies are tested by a certified technician at installation and at least annually, and the tester uploads results to BSI Online (bsionline.com).

Source: library.municode.com

Are there lawn replacement or sprinkler rebates in Evans?

  • Slow the Flow sprinkler evaluations (Resource Central) — Free sprinkler evaluation with a custom watering schedule; some participants may be eligible for a new smart controller. Sign up on the 2026 waitlist at resourcecentral.org/sprinklers. evanscolorado.gov
  • Garden in a Box (Resource Central) — $25 discounted water-wise garden kits for Evans residents; 2026 sales opened March 4 through resourcecentral.org/gardens. evanscolorado.gov

Also worth knowing in Evans

  • State law requires a utility locate before digging: call 811 (or UNCC at 1-800-922-1987) and allow at least 48 hours before you dig. evanscolorado.gov
  • Tree Assistance Program: the City may reimburse up to $275 for an approved tree purchase (one per household per year); 2026 applications run April 1 to October 1, and trees bought before approval don't qualify. evanscolorado.gov
  • Evans does not issue contractor licenses, but anyone doing business in city limits needs a City business license. evanscolorado.gov
